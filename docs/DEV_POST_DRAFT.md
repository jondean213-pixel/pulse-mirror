## What I Built
Pulse Mirror: a mirror that beats with your heart. Look into your phone and a ring around
your reflection pulses at your real heart rate, a halo breathes with you, and the colour
shifts from coral to mint as you settle.

## How I Built It
Kotlin + SmartSpectra Android SDK 3.5.0 (API-key setup). Live signals: pulse rate, chest
breathing trace and rate, HRV RMSSD, expressions, and validation status for coaching hints.
Custom `PulseRingView` turns the numbers into motion.
